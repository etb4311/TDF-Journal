# Assignment:

I. For this week, the assignment was to build an AI app in Gemini Studio. I made a coffee-catching game, inspired by an idea my boss (who I work with at a coffee shop) floated about building an arcade cabinet, since he has woodworking experience and I'm interested in game development. - Since this initial pass didn't quite work, I decided to make another app related to my studio foundations project

# Reflections:

My first reaction to the OpenAI "automated research intern" milestone is skepticism, it feels like marketing more than substance. Citing things like work-day ratios and experiment velocity measures throughput, but says nothing about the quality of the research being produced. The real question is always whether something is being done, versus being done well, with intention and actual impact.

That skepticism connects to the Cybernetic Forest glossary too. I think we're deep in the age of the buzzword right now, and it's worth staying alert to what people actually mean when they use this language, if they mean anything specific at all. A lot of it is empty language designed to generate hype.

On the more hands-on side, building the coffee game was a good lesson in how fast AI tools can produce something functional, but also how flat that output can be without deliberate direction. The fact that vague prompts across the class converged on similar-looking UI reinforced that specificity is what separates a generic AI output from something with actual character.
