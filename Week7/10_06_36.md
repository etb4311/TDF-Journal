### Digital Twin Stages/Layers

**1. Animals + Sensors (Input of real world objects)**

**2. Digital Twin**
The data is used to build a digital representation that reflects the characteristics and behavior of the real system.
**3. Live Data**
New data continuously updates the digital twin so it reflects changing real-world conditions.
**4. Analysis + Simulation**
The twin can be used to test 'what-if' scenarios, identify patterns, predict outcomes, and evaluate changes.
**5. Conservation Decisions**
Insight from the twin can guide real-world decisions, improve performance, reduce risk, and support future planning.
